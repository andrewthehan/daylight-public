Privacy Policy — Daylight

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Daylight is a daily puzzle game. This policy explains what data it does and doesn't collect.

No account, no personal data collection

Daylight doesn't require an account and doesn't collect your name, email, or any other personal information.

What's stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, streak, and app settings are stored locally on your device only. This data is never sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Puzzle content

The app fetches the daily puzzle from a static file host (daylight.ahh.fyi). That request doesn't include any personal information, though the hosting provider may keep standard web server logs (such as IP address) as part of its own infrastructure — this is outside our control and not something Daylight itself collects or has access to.

Purchases

Daylight offers optional in-app purchases (subscriptions and a lifetime unlock). These are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and purchase/entitlement state is managed for us by RevenueCat, a third-party subscription platform. We don't receive your payment details.

Sharing

When you share a result or a custom puzzle, Daylight hands the content to your device's native share sheet. We don't see or store who you shared with or how.

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking

Daylight has no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support+daylight@ahh.fyi
