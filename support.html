Need help with Daylight? Email support+daylight@ahh.fyi and we'll get back to you.

How to play

Tap a tile to flip it and the tiles directly above, below, left, and right of it. Match the target shape shown above the board to win. Some puzzles also award bonus stars for lighting every tile, or going fully dark.

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings in the app and use the "Restore Purchases" option. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID or Google account, not to a Daylight account (there isn't one).
